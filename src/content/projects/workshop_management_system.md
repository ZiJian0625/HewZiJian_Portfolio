## Overview
Designed for Greenstem Business Software Sdn Bhd, this app gives workshop clients a smooth, hassle-free digital experience. <br><br>Built with Flutter and powered by Firebase, it replaces manual calls and paperwork by letting users schedule services, follow live repair statuses, handle e-payments, and drop quick feedback straight from their phones.

## Features
- **Service Booking**: Step-by-step online booking with vehicle selection, custom time picker, and auto-suggested nearest workshop locations.
- **Real-time Progress Tracking**: Real-time updates on vehicle repair status with technician notes and photos.
- **E-Billing & Payment**: View digital invoices, calculate dynamic overdue charges, and process simulated online card payments.
- **Service Schedule**: View upcoming and past service records.
- **Push Notification**: Alerts customers about upcoming schedules, completed services, and overdue 
payment.
- **Quality Feedback**: Star rating and review system for completed services and overall workshop experience.
- **Vehicle Management**: Full vehicle profile management with photo uploads.
- **Secure Authentication**: Secure login, registration, password reset, and encrypted "Remember Me" credentials.
- **Direct Workshop Support**: One-tap WhatsApp button for direct support messaging.
